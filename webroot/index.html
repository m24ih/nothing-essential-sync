ABOUT THIS ENGINE

Essential Sync is an ultra-lightweight, zero-battery bridge for Nothing OS.

  • 100% Read-Only Safety: Uses Snap-to-RAM snapshotting. Never writes to or alters Nothing OS databases.
  • Event-Driven: Driven by Linux inotify. Consumes 0.0% CPU and zero battery while idle.
  • Native Obsidian Format: Extracts text, images (.webp), audio recordings (.wav), and AI transcripts cleanly into Markdown.
